Examples

  • Wo ist die Brotdose? Ich finde sie nicht. Where is the lunch box? I can't find it.
  • Es regnet. Es ist schon spät. It's raining. It's already late.

Notes

es is for das words. For der and die words, it is er and sie. es also starts sentences about weather and time.
