in

in / into

A1 assistant

Part of speech
preposition
Case
Akkusativ (where to?) or Dativ (where?)
Grammar point

Examples

  • Wir gehen in die Halle. We are going into the hall.
  • Wir sind in der Halle. We are in the hall.

Notes

in die Halle: where to, Akkusativ. in der Halle: where, Dativ.
