# sich

## Examples

- Die Kinder freuen sich. :: The children are happy.
- Die Eltern treffen sich am Donnerstag. :: The parents meet on Thursday.

## Notes

The reflexive pronoun of *er, sie, es, sie* (plural) and *Sie*. The others are *mich, dich, uns, euch*.
