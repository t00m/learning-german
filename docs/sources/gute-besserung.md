# Gute Besserung!

## Variants

- Gute Besserung an deinen Sohn! :: Get well soon to your son!
- Gute Besserung für Ihre Tochter! :: Get well soon to your daughter!

## Usage

What you say to someone who is ill, or about someone who is ill.
