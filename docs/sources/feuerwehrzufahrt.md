# Feuerwehrzufahrt

![A round white sign with a red ring above a sign for the fire brigade access](resources/photos/2026-08-29-feuerwehrzufahrt.jpg)

## Transcription

Feuerwehrzufahrt

Zuschauereingang

Gas

## Meaning

The round white sign with a red ring means: no vehicles of any kind (*Verbot für Fahrzeuge aller Art*).
The sign below says why: this is the access road for the fire brigade (*Feuerwehrzufahrt*). It must always stay clear, so never park or stop here.
The small yellow sign marks a gas valve under the ground. On the wall, an arrow shows the way to the spectators' entrance (*Zuschauereingang*).
