# der

## Examples

- Der Trainer schreibt alles in den Chat. :: The coach writes everything in the chat.

## Notes

Masculine: *der* (Nominativ), *den* (Akkusativ), *dem* (Dativ). *der* is also the Dativ of *die*: *mit der Mannschaft*.
