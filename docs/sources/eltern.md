# Eltern

## Examples

- Die Eltern treffen sich am Donnerstag in der Schule. :: The parents meet at school on Thursday.

## Notes

Only plural: *die Eltern*. One parent is *der Elternteil*, but people usually say *Mutter* or *Vater*.
