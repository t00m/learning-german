# Privatgrundstück

![A white sign of the district on a pole in front of a sports hall](resources/photos/2026-08-29-privatgrundstueck.jpg)

## Transcription

Privatgrundstück

Unbefugtes Betreten und Befahren verboten!

Unbefugtes Parken auf eigene Gefahr!

Eingeschränkter Winterdienst!

[Landkreis]

## Meaning

Private property. Entering on foot or by car without permission is forbidden.
If you park here without permission, you do so at your own risk: the owner is not responsible for damage and may have your car towed.
The snow and ice are only partly cleared in winter (*Winterdienst* is the clearing service).
