Notes

  • Standards in football are set pieces: free kicks, corners, throw-ins.
  • locker here means light, relaxed. keine großartigen ... Dinge is colloquial for "nothing big".
  • für alle zur Info tells you the coach answers one parent but writes for the whole group.
