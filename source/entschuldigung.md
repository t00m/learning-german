# Entschuldigung

## Examples

- Muss ich eine Entschuldigung schreiben? :: Do I have to write an excuse note?

## Notes

Alone, *Entschuldigung!* means *Excuse me!* or *Sorry!*. At school, *eine Entschuldigung* is the note that explains why a child was absent.
