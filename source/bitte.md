# bitte

## Examples

- Noch fünf Minuten, bitte! :: Five more minutes, please!
- Ja, bitte. Was gibt's? :: Yes, please. What is it?

## Notes

*Bitte?* alone means *Pardon?*. *Bitte!* also answers *Danke!*
