# Treffen

## Examples

- Treffen um 18.15 Uhr in Ort C! :: Meeting at 18:15 in Town C!
- Das Treffen ist eine Stunde vor dem Anpfiff. :: The meeting is one hour before kick-off.

## Notes

In a match message *Treffen* is when the players must be there, not when the match starts. The noun is the infinitive of *sich treffen*, so it is neuter.
