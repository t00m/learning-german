# Beeil dich!

## Variants

- Beeilt euch, der Bus kommt! :: Hurry up, the bus is coming! (to several)
- Wir müssen uns beeilen. :: We have to hurry.

## Usage

To a child in the morning. *sich beeilen* is reflexive.
