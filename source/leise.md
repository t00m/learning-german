# leise

## Examples

- Bitte seid leise, das Baby schläft. :: Please be quiet, the baby is sleeping.
- Ich mache die Musik sofort leiser. :: I'll turn the music down right away.

## Notes

The opposite is *laut*. *leiser machen*: to turn down.
