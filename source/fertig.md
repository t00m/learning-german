# fertig

## Examples

- Bist du fertig? Wir müssen los! :: Are you ready? We have to go!
- Ich mache die Präsentation morgen fertig. :: I'll finish the presentation tomorrow.

## Notes

*fertig sein*: to be ready or finished. *fertig machen*: to finish something.
