Examples

  • Das Halteverbot gilt für alle, ausgenommen Linienbusse. :: The no-stopping rule applies to everyone except scheduled buses.
  • Die Durchfahrt ist verboten, Anlieger ausgenommen. :: No through traffic, residents excepted.

Notes

Very common on extra plates under traffic signs. It can come before or after the noun. Anlieger frei says the same thing for residents.
