Privatgrundstück

Unbefugtes Betreten und Befahren verboten!

Unbefugtes Parken auf eigene Gefahr!

Eingeschränkter Winterdienst!

[Landkreis]

Meaning

Private property. Entering on foot or by car without permission is forbidden. If you park here without permission, you do so at your own risk: the owner is not responsible for damage and may have your car towed. The snow and ice are only partly cleared in winter (Winterdienst is the clearing service).
