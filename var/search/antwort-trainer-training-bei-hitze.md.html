Hallo, ist ok, für alle zur Info:

Hello, that's OK. For everyone's information:

Wir werden nur lockere Übungen machen und ein paar Standards, keine großartigen anstrengenden Dinge.

We will only do light exercises and a few set pieces, nothing very strenuous.

Gruß

Regards

Notes

  • Standards in football are set pieces: free kicks, corners, throw-ins.
  • locker here means light, relaxed. keine großartigen ... Dinge is colloquial for "nothing big".
  • für alle zur Info tells you the coach answers one parent but writes for the whole group.
