Transcription

an Schultagen von 7-9h von 11-16h

ausgenommen Linienbusse

Meaning

Three signs on one pole:

  • The red triangle with two children: watch out, children (a school is near).
  • The blue sign with a red cross: absolute no stopping (absolutes Halteverbot). You may not even stop for a moment. The arrow shows the direction in which the ban applies from the sign.
  • The extra plate (Zusatzzeichen): the ban only applies on school days, from 7 to 9 and from 11 to 16 o'clock. Scheduled public buses (Linienbusse) are allowed to stop.
