Examples

  • An Schultagen ist hier von 7 bis 9 Uhr Halteverbot. :: On school days there is no stopping here from 7 to 9.
  • Das Halteverbot gilt nicht für Linienbusse. :: The no-stopping rule does not apply to scheduled buses.

Notes

Halten is stopping for up to three minutes, Parken is longer or leaving the car. A red cross on blue is the absolute ban (absolutes Halteverbot); a single red stripe is the limited one (eingeschränktes Halteverbot), where you may stop for three minutes but not park.
